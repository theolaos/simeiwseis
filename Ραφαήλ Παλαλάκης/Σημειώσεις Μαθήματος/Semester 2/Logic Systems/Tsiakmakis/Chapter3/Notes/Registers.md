___
Registers are a series of **Flip-Flops** that all share the **same clock**.
The output of each flip-flop continues on, on the next flip-flop input.
The circuit, moves the series of bits, one step on each clock.

We have :
- Left
- Right
- Both Ways
- Circular

___
### Types Of Registers


##### Serial & Parallel Registers

- Serial-In-Serial-Out (SISO)
- Serial-In-Parallel-Out(SIPO)
- Parallel-In-Parallel-Out(PIPO)
- Parallel-In-Serial-Out(PISO)

##### Circular Registers

- Johnson
- Daktiliou
